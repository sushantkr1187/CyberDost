vpn_key_alert The eMudhra TLS Incident & Web Trust Infrastructure Scrutiny

In 2024–2025, security research and CA/Browser Forum audits exposed critical weaknesses in Certificate Authority key generation, predictable PRNG random seeds, and delayed Certificate Transparency (CT) logging at eMudhra. This demonstrated that having an active HTTPS padlock does not guarantee safety. Weak cryptographic parameters can allow fraudulent certificates to be created for website impersonation.

password 1. Weak PRNG & Keygen Flaws

Subscriber keys generated with flawed random seeds produce RSA primes vulnerable to GCD batch factorization or Coppersmith's attack (ROCA pattern).

history_toggle_off 2. Delayed CT Logging

Failure to promptly append Signed Certificate Timestamps (SCTs) to public CT logs prevents domain owners from monitoring unauthorized certificate issuance in real-time.

rule 3. CAA Record Absence

Without strict DNS Certification Authority Authorization (CAA) records, any compromised or flawed CA worldwide can issue certificates for high-value banking and government domains.
